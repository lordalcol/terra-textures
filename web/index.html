My first OpenGL program (2009), in WebGL

Layers

Keys: 1–6 toggle the layers, Space stops the rotation. Drag the globe to turn it.

About

I wrote the original in C++ with OpenGL and GLUT in 2009, for the Elementi di grafica digitale exam at the University of Trieste. It layers the textures with multitexturing: each layer gets its own texture unit, and each unit multiplies the colour by its texture.

This version does the same in one WebGL fragment shader. The sphere is built the same way, as rings of triangle strips with the texture coordinates taken from longitude and latitude, and the clouds are blended on top so thick clouds come out white.
